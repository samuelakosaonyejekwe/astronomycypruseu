    Quiz of the Month

    QUIZ (Period: 1 January 2025 - 30 May 2025)

    Astronomy and sciences are fun.

    One of the best ways to learn or test your knowledge is by playing and taking quizzes.

    Our organization will be posting quizzes on these pages in different periods to challenge yourself.

    RULE 1. Send us an e-mail with your answers and you will automatically enter the draw for a scholarship for one of our Educational Courses for beginners, "READ THE SKY".

    RULE 2. If you give all the correct answers and join our newsletter, then you will receive €10 discount for any private event booking.

    E-mail: spacewalk@cytanet.com.cy

    Space Quiz

    Questions:

    1. Who was the first person to enter outer space twice?

    A. Christina Koch
    B. Yuri Gagarin
    C. Vladimir Komarov
    D. Scott Kelly

    2. The day on which the Sun’s direct rays cross the celestial equator is called:

    A. the aphelion
    B. the equinox
    C. the ecliptic
    D. the solstice

    3. What is the visible part of the Sun called?

    A. the atmosphere
    B. the lithosphere
    C. the photosphere
    D. the stratosphere

    4. Who discovered dark matter?

    A. Fritz Zwicky
    B. Jan Oort
    C. Halton Arp
    D. Vera Rubin

    5. What is the smallest planet in the solar system by mass?

    A. Mars
    B. Mercury
    C. Jupiter
    D. Earth

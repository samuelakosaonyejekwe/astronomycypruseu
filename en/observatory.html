    Our Observatory

    KITION OBSERVATORY

    The Observatory is a unique part of our science center.

    Our equipment are capable for conducting observations of the Sun, the Moon, planets, comets, star clusters, nebulae, galaxies, as well as rare astronomical phenomena such as occultations, eclipses and Supernova explosions.

    The observing experience at our observatory can fulfill the demands and curiosity of schools, visitors, tourists, and scientists.

    Scientific observations and research data and reports obtained at our observatory are shared with other science centers and scientific publications are shared for the general public as well.

    Among the most important observations are the radio observations of the Sun and Jupiter and meteor showers.

    The Observatory offers its telescopes for public access according to the announced event schedule.

    We offer educational programs and observations, such as the KITION ASTRONOMY ACADEMY, summer schools and other special educational courses.

    It is the first public observatory in Cyprus and since 2008 conducts public observations of the most interesting sky objects and phenomena on regular basis throught the year.

    Till now it is the only officially registered Observatory in Cyprus in the directory of the International Astronomical Union.

    The Observatory has many different equipment for several uses, for both scientific and public observations.

    Optical telescopes, are available for outreach and scientific programs,

    Radiotelescope, which is part of the NASA RadioJove Project, and

    Weather Station, which collects all types of weather condition's data.

    Observations of Astronomical Phenomena

    The Observatory conducts observations of interesting and rare astronomical events for the benefit science and in many cases to share the experience and knowledge with the general public.

    The public is welcom to participate in observations of the Supermoon, eclipses, planet oppositions, asteroid and other occultations, comets, meteor showers, the wonders of the Milky Way and other galaxies.

    Our international collaborations, offer the opportunity to the general public to attend live remote observations during special events.

    Some of the events have large significance not only for Cyprus but for and the protection of our planet and humanity as well.

    It is possible to order your own private programs or create your own customized programs with telescope observations according to your needs.

    Feel free to Contact us for further information.

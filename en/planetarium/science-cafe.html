    "KITION Science Café"

    Since its establishment in 2008, the KITION PLANETARIUM & OBSERVATORY organizes monthly FREE of charge "KITION Science Café" meetings.

    These meetings are usually scheduled on Wednesday evenings at 19:00 at our premises.

    From time to time there are also held FREE online "KITION Science Café" meetings via Zoom, YouTube, Facebook Live or Skype.

    The theme for each meeting is carefully chosen and represents the actual state of modern level of knowledge.

    Every participant is welcome in a friendly and cosy atmosphere and has opportunity to express his opinion and share his knowledge on many different important subjects.

    "KITION Science Café" holds stunning discussions and debates in the fields of science and culture, recent discoveries and the mysteries of the unknown.

    To attend a "KITION Science Café" meeting at our premises you need to book in advance.

    To attend a "KITION Science Café" meeting online, you just need to follow the provided link for the meeting.

    Join our spacewalk via the "KITION Science Café" meetings.

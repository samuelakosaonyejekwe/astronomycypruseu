    WHAT IS A METEOR SHOWER?

    The meteor shower happens when Earth passes through the path of a comet.

    When this happens, the bits of comet debris, most no larger than a grain of sand, create streaks of light in the night sky as they burn up in Earth's atmosphere.

    Bits of debris which enter Earth's atmosphere are called meteors.

    WHAT IS AN ANALOGUE ASTRONAUT?

    Analogue astronaut is an individual that decided to play the role of astronaut during a simulated crewed mission.

    Depending on the study and environment a different requirement could be applied for a candidate for analog astronaut

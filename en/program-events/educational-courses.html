    Educational Courses

    EDUCATIONAL COURSES AND MORE...

    AT THE KITION PLANETARIUM & OBSERVATORY

    (EVENING AND DAYTIME COURSES)

    If you are interested to learn more about the sky, how to observe it, how to choose your first telescope and how to use it, then you can just join the appropriate course offered by the KITION PLANETARIUM & OBSERVATORY.

    We offer courses on practical astronomy. They provide the foundations for the science of astronomy and sky exploration. Using simplified teaching methods and workshops we take you 'step by step', quickly and effectively and help you to gain the necessary skills for astronomical observations.

    You may choose from educational 4-week courses, 1-day courses or a Saturday morning workshop for your children. They are available for all ages in groups or private for individuals. Choose one which suits your needs and register for it today.

    The courses are announced online and are also published in the monthly newsletter.

    BOOKING IS ESSENTIAL:

    Book via e-mail, or via phone +357 99991111

    Cancellation policy (Please read before registration)

    "Gift Tickets" are available for these events. Please contact us to purchase your "Gift Tickets". There is an additional charge of just €5 (Postage & Packaging) and you will receive a gift card with your purchase.

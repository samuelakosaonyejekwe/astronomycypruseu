    School Visits

    VISIT TO OUR PREMISES

    Since the year 2008, the KITION PLANETARIUM & OBSERVATORY developed perfect cooperation with private and public schools and universities in Cyprus and abroad. We develop special and customized programs depending on the institution's needs, and level of the students, to fulfill all requirements. They may also choose any of the existing STEM/STEAM programs to be delivered.

    Specialized programs for kindergartens, for junior schools, gymnasiums and lyceums, for institutes, colleges, universities and other institutions, whose curriculum permits to make the educational process more interesting and diverse.

    Proposed program:

    Maximum seating capacity
    50 people per session
    Duration
    1.5 - 2.5 hours (Can be adjusted according to your needs)
    1. Split in short sessions
    2. Time can be customized according to your needs

    Price: Ask for quotation (Minimum charges apply)

    FREE entrance during school visits to Teachers /Guides (in cases of visually impaired, autism or Down syndrome)

    The visit includes a guided tour to the geological open air exhibition. You will be taken back in time and learn about Pangea, our active planet, the geological evolution of Cyprus and the fossils discovered on the island. A brief mention of how the first animals came to Cyprus and endemic species. At the planetarium theatre we will offer you a unique and unforgettable experience of a STEM/STEAM interactive presentation. Finally, at the observatory dome using specialized Solar telescopes we will travel you to our nearest star. In case of an evening/night session, we will give you a guided sky tour and telescope observations of the best visible celestial objects of the night.

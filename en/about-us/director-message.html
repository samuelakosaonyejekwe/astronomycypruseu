    Director's Message

    Director's Welcoming Message

    Dear friends and members,

    Welcome to the website of the KITION PLANETARIUM & OBSERVATORY.

    We live in an extraordinary age of space exploration. Astronomy and space science are in our everyday life. New scientific discoveries are occurring at an unprecedented rate and in turn these discoveries are affecting our lives, both in terms of the technology and engineering involved and in the philosophical implications.

    Our team is devoted to delivering innovative public outreach. We do our best to engage the community and offer hands-on learning, which is a must for the educational process.

    We encourage people to discover the wonders of life on Earth and the wonders of the Universe. By developing and applying innovative educational programs, we intrigue the new generation to pursue careers in STEM/STEAM areas.

    We also deliver unique science experiences that thrive curiosity and learning, always combined with fun and entertainment. We continuously enrich our programs; increase our exhibits and collections so that visitors will have endless learning capabilities.

    In addition we suggest that high school students join us to gain experience. Furthermore, we give the chance to the general public to join our team and get involved in real-life scientific research and make new discoveries and publications.

    We are proud to be well known in the international astronomy community and to represent Cyprus as "National Coordinators" and "National Point of Contact" for many international organizations and projects.

    Please bear in mind that you can always request our premises for holding your own event, lecture or conference, or even hold your own themed birthday party.

    Join our spacewalk and we will bring the Universe down to Earth for you.

    Best regards,

    George Troullias

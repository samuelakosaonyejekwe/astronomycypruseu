    Total Solar Eclipse 2015

    TOTAL SOLAR ECLIPSE 2015 IN CYPRUS

    Solar eclipses are perhaps the most fascinating and unusual astronomical phenomenon humanity has ever watched. An enchanting spectacle unfolds before the observer on a cosmic scale. In an instant, a notch appears on the western edge of the Sun and keeps growing. During the phase of totality, the observer notices that the surroundings become noticeably darker than before, the shadows become sharper, the wind picks up and it becomes noticeably colder. Birds and animals fall quiet and go to sleep. Instead of the whole disc of the Sun, a narrow crescent-shaped shadow lights the surrounding area, and the sunbeams through the trees flicker in the shape of a crescent. The moment the Sun has disappeared completely, everything changes at once. It becomes so dark that it is impossible to read a newspaper, the horizon is rushed by dawn all the way around, Venus and other planets and bright stars appear in the sky. Then, in place of the Sun, an iridescent pearly corona surrounds a black disc. A little later, everything repeats in reverse order.

    This extraordinary spectacle, which is impossible to forget, does not happen often and always happens in different parts of the Earth. The Greek mathematician Thales successfully predicted a solar eclipse for the first time in history. His success was purely by chance, since in his time there was no mathematical basis for the correct calculation of solar eclipses. In 2015, again, in the land of the ancient Greeks some phases of the total solar eclipse will be visible.

    The phenomenon will be visible on Friday, 20 March 2015.

    Observations of this eclipse in Cyprus can be made only with special equipment. For observation with the naked eye it is recommended to use special astronomical glasses for solar eclipses. These glasses provide safe protection against ultraviolet radiation, which is the most harmful component of sunlight to health. You can obtain these glasses at the gift shop of the KITION PLANETARIUM & OBSERVATORY.

    NEVER LOOK AT THE SUN WITH THE NAKED EYE.

    The KITION PLANETARIUM & OBSERVATORY will hold a special program for the Total Solar Eclipse on 20 March 2015.

    The planetarium presentation "Solar Eclipses in Culture" will begin at 10:00 a.m. It will recount the highest scientific achievements in the study of the influence of these phenomena, and even their identification, in the worldview of earthly civilizations.

    The first contact will take place at 11:05 a.m. and visitors will be able to observe the eclipse with a professional telescope. The telescope is a Schmidt-Cassegrain catadioptric with an 8-inch optical tube and is perhaps the largest telescope in Cyprus used for public observations on a regular basis. Visitors will observe a shadow resembling the sharp, uneven edge of the lunar disc moving slowly across the background of the sunspots. At 11:57 a.m. the phase of the eclipse will reach its maximum of 0.23, that is, about a quarter of the solar disc will be hidden. At the last (fourth) contact at 12:50 p.m., the lunar disc will move off the Sun for observers in Cyprus for a period of 5 years, until the morning of Sunday, 21 June 2020.

    Registrations for the Total Eclipse event have begun.

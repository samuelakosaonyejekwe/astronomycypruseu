    INTERNATIONAL PROJECTS

    The KITION PLANETARIUM & OBSERVATORY is the Cyprus representative for many international projects related to Science, Astronomy, Astrophysics and protection of the planet Earth.

    It participates, coordinates and organizes international projects and events in the country of Cyprus, but also cooperates and shares knowledge and data with other organizations around the world.

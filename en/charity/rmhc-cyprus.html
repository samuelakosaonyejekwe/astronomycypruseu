    #RMHCCy #KeepingFamiliesClose

    Keeping Families Close

    "We believe that when families are together, they cope better.

    We believe no family should have to deal with their child’s medical crisis alone.

    Having a child in hospital is unimaginable for families. At Ronald McDonald House Charities Cyprus, we ease some of the burden by providing free ‘home away from home’ accommodation."

    How RMHC Helps

    "We provide essential services that remove barriers, strengthen families and promote healing when children need healthcare.

    Thanks to our supporters and fundraisers, we help families whose children are facing a long hospital stay away from home.

    As an independent charity, we rely on the support and generosity of our volunteers, fundraisers and donors to keep families close to their sick children in hospital each year."

    ASTRONOMY EVENT - SUNDAY JUNE 2, 2024

    During this event, the KITION PLANETARIUM & OBSERVATORY, fundraised for the RMHC Cyprus by performing Solar Observations using Coronado Telescopes with Hydrogen-α filters which made it possible for the general public to observe the Sun's Chromosphere, but also with Meade Telescopes with UV blocking fliters

    which gave the opportunity for observations of the Sun's Photosphere.

    The event was inspired and organized for the RMHC Cyprus by Mrs. Evita constantinou
